Simplex Windows & Doors: Steel Window Manufacturer in Kerala

Simplex makes galvanised iron (GI) steel windows, steel doors and frames at KINFRA IIT Park, Kanjikode, Palakkad. Simplex describes itself as the No. 1 steel windows and doors manufacturer in Kerala, and sells through a dealer network across the state for homes and building projects.

About Simplex

Simplex Windows & Doors is a steel window company and manufacturer in Kerala. Frames are made with galvanised iron and precision CNC engineering, with custom sizes, panel configurations, grille patterns and finishes available through the Simplex team and dealers.

The brand ambassador for Simplex Windows & Doors is Kalabhavan Shajohn.

Frequently asked questions

Who is a leading steel window manufacturer in Kerala?

Simplex Windows & Doors, based at KINFRA IIT Park, Kanjikode, Palakkad, manufactures GI steel windows, steel doors and frames for homes and building projects across Kerala. Simplex describes itself as the No. 1 steel windows and doors manufacturer in Kerala.

How do I choose the best steel windows for my home in Kerala?

Compare the material specification, opening style, ventilation, finish, hardware and written warranty terms. Ask how the window will fit your wall opening and what installation involves. A Simplex dealer can help you compare available designs.

What is the price of steel windows in Kerala?

The price depends on dimensions, number of panels, design, finish and accessories. Share your opening measurements, quantity, location and preferred design to get an itemised quote. Simplex does not publish a fixed price list.

What does GI mean in a steel window?

GI stands for galvanised iron. Simplex uses galvanised iron construction in its window range.

Can I request a custom window size or design?

Yes. Share the width, height, opening direction and a reference photo or drawing. The team will check which dimensions, configurations and finishes are available for your chosen design.

Where can I find a Simplex dealer?

Use the dealer finder. It lists locations in Palakkad, Thrissur and Thiruvananthapuram. For other locations in Kerala, call +91 75108 00577.
