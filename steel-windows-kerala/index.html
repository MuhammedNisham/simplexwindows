HELP WITH YOUR DECISION

Steel window questions, answered

How do I choose the best steel windows for my home in Kerala?

Compare the material specification, opening style, ventilation, finish, hardware and written warranty terms. Ask how the quoted window will fit your wall opening and what installation involves. The best choice depends on your room, budget and site conditions; our dealer can help you compare the available Simplex designs.

What does GI mean in a steel window?

GI stands for galvanised iron. Simplex uses galvanised iron construction in its window range. When comparing options, ask for the material, finish and maintenance requirements of the specific model you intend to order.

Can I request a custom window size or design?

Share the width, height, opening direction and a reference photo or drawing with our team. We will help you check which dimensions, configurations and finishes are available for your chosen design before you order.
