PRICES AND SPECIFICATIONS

What affects a steel door quote?

Dimensions, panel design, frame specification, finish and hardware all need to be defined before you compare quotes. This website does not publish a fixed door price list.

  1. Choose a reference. Select a catalogue design or share a picture of the style you have in mind.
  2. Provide measurements. Send the opening width and height, quantity, location and preferred opening direction. Confirm final measurements before manufacture.
  3. Check the full specification. Ask about the frame, door panel, lock, handle, finish and any site-specific requirements.
  4. Review the written quote. Confirm what is included in the price, delivery and fitting arrangements, lead time and warranty terms.

HELP WITH YOUR DECISION

Steel door questions, answered

Can I choose a door and frame together?

Yes, you can discuss a steel door design and a frame option with Simplex. Ask the team to confirm that the selected dimensions, profile, hardware and opening direction work together before placing an order.

What should I compare when choosing the best steel door?

Compare the specification and suitability for the intended opening, the finish, frame, locks, handles, installation arrangements and written warranty. A decorative appearance alone does not describe the full door specification.

Can I ask for a customised steel door?

Send your measurements, preferred panel style, finish and site details. Our team can confirm available customisation and pricing for the selected design.
