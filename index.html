WHO WE ARE

Simplex is Kerala's premier manufacturer of high-quality, premium steel windows and doors. We are dedicated to bringing any custom design or unique requirement to life, strictly on time. Our precision manufacturing process guarantees absolutely no compromise on structural quality and finish.

We proudly supply and deliver our premium steel products to residential and commercial projects all across India. For our local clients, we offer the added benefit of completely free delivery anywhere within the state of Kerala. Beyond our craftsmanship, we are best known for our exceptional and highly reliable after-sales service.
